Our point of view

She has many roles. Indra is made for all of them.

Work can mean a team, a classroom, a clinic, a studio, a shop, a home office or a business of her own. Indra is here for the everyday rhythm around it all, with clothing made to feel comfortable, versatile and distinctly hers.

Manapattayil Textiles began its journey in Kanchiyar, Kerala, in 1980. Indra carries that textile heritage into a new generation of women's fashion.

Indra is the name you wear. Manapattayil Textiles is the heritage behind it.
