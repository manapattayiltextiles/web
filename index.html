Meet Indra

Modern style, with heritage behind it.

Indra is the first modern fashion brand from Manapattayil Textiles, a textile business established in Kanchiyar, Kerala, in 1980.

Thoughtful, versatile clothing for women moving between work, family, ambition and the moments that are simply their own.
